Know what you're signing before you sign it.

Paste or upload a lease, sublease, or notice. LeaseLens figures out what kind of document it is, flags what's missing or worth asking about, and answers your questions using only the text in front of you.

This gives you information about your document, not legal advice. For anything you're unsure about, a tenant rights clinic or attorney can confirm your specific situation.
